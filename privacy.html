Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what information CLIMB OR BUST collects and how it's used. If anything here is unclear, feel free to reach out — contact details are at the bottom.

1. What we collect directly

When you start the game, you're asked for a display name. This name, along with your game progress (stage, items, mastery levels, etc.), is stored locally in your own browser using standard web storage. It is not transmitted to any server we operate, and we have no way to see, collect, or sell it. Clearing your browser data or using a different browser/device will start a fresh save.

If the optional World Leaderboard feature is active, your chosen display name and best stage reached may be shown publicly to other players. Don't use your real name if you'd rather not.

2. Advertising and cookies

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense and its advertising partners. These third-party vendors use cookies and similar technologies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also visit aboutads.info to opt out of participating vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising.

3. Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall traffic (e.g., how many people visit, which pages are popular). This data is aggregated and not tied to your identity.

4. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we'll remove it.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally as the site changes. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the contact info on the developer's blog, linked from the game's About page.
